## Method

Certified means: evaluated on the published tests for that capability, on a named model, with 95 percent confidence that at least 85 percent of runs pass (57 of the newest 60 for a single test), no single test below 80 percent, and the record published. The lower bound is the 95 percent Wilson bound on the newest 60 graded runs; runs marked not applicable or infrastructure are left out. A forbidden action fails its run whatever else happened. The checks are deterministic; the judge's scores are stored and never decide a pass. Source: `bench/run-store/runs/`, `scripts/eval_dashboard/certification_statement.py`.
