Certified capabilities

What kube-agents can and cannot do today, by model. A row is a capability in plain words; its status is computed from the tests that are its evidence, from the evaluation record. Nothing on this page is typed by hand except the row names.

What certified means

Certified means: evaluated on the published tests for that capability, on a named model, with 95 percent confidence that at least 85 percent of runs pass (57 of the newest 60 for a single test), no single test below 80 percent, and the record published.

Guaranteed

The agent can only propose a change, never apply one: read-only access rights and a gateway that blocks writes. The row above counts cluster or repository writes caught by a safeguard across each record.

How to read the rows

Green needs 95 percent confidence of at least 85 percent pass on the row, and no test inside it below 80 percent. A short record cannot reach it: three runs per test is one night. The percentage is the plain pass rate. A ⏱ marks a capability that runs on a schedule with no person in the loop; none is graded on its scheduled result yet, so its evidence comes from the same work requested by a test.

Method

Section titled “Method”

Rows: bench/run-store/capabilities.yaml. Numbers: bench/run-store/runs/, the newest 60 graded runs per test on the model; runs marked not applicable are left out; the lower bound is the 95 percent Wilson bound. Generators: scripts/eval_dashboard/capability_checklist.py and render_checklist.py --mdx.
